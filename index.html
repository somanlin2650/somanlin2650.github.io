No View Is the Whole

The World and the Ways We Make Sense of It

Soman Lin

The book starts from a few scenes you have probably been in: a meeting where everybody insists they are stating facts; a model that performed beautifully in its old environment and failed quietly somewhere new; an argument where you understood every sentence and still felt the two of you lived in different worlds.

Eleven chapters in three parts, with thirteen diagrams. Chinese and English, switchable.

所見非全貌

世界，以及我們理解它的方式

Soman Lin

這本書從幾個每天都會遇到的場面開始：一場每個人都說自己在講事實的會議；一套在舊環境裡表現漂亮、換個地方就悄悄失效的模型；一場你聽懂了對方每一句話、卻仍然覺得你們住在不同世界的爭吵。

全書十一章分成三部，十三張圖。中英雙語，可切換。
