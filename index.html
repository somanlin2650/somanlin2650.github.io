No View Is the Whole

The World and the Ways We Make Sense of It

Soman Lin

The book opens on five scenes with nothing in common: physicians in 1854 who believed cholera travelled through the smell in the air; an army scoring ten thousand hamlets a month and drifting further from the war it was trying to understand; an autopilot handing control back while the panel went on producing numbers; a child written into the box marked “hyperactive”; a message that goes three hours without a reply.

Fourteen chapters in four parts, with thirteen diagrams. Chinese and English, switchable.

所見非全貌

這本書從五個彼此無關的場面開始：一八五四年相信霍亂來自空氣裡臭味的醫師；每個月替上萬個村落打分、報表越完整卻離戰爭越遠的軍隊；把控制權交還給組員、儀表卻繼續流暢輸出數字的自動駕駛；一個被寫進「過動」那一格的孩子；一則三小時沒有回音的訊息。

全書十四章分成四部，十三張圖。中英雙語，可切換。
