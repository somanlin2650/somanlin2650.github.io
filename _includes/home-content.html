How does knowledge become useful understanding? Essays by Soman Lin explore that question through AI and software systems, cognition, learning, and philosophy.

From the blind spots of an automated system to the assumptions behind an everyday judgment, the concern is practical: how to understand a situation, act within it, and revise the method when it falls short.

Explore the questions

  • Cognition, civilization, and AI — How reflex, intuition, and reason form one continuum, and what changes if AI builds primitives of its own.
  • AI agents and automation — Why a system needs oversight beyond its own view of the world.
  • Self-directed learning and understanding — How inherited knowledge becomes an ability to reason independently.
  • Moral judgment and reflection — How shared norms can guide action while remaining open to revision.
